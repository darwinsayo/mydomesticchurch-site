What is inside

Everything your household needs, nothing it doesn't

Every feature has its own On/Off switch, so your family sees only what you choose. Screens show sample family data.

Prayer

A prayer book in English and Latin with search and favorites, a guided Rosary, and a family prayer list. The liturgical season and feast days appear through the app, with a button to the day's Mass readings.

School

Courses for each child spread over your school days, with assignments, due dates and a week view. Each child sees only their own work, and parents see who is on pace or behind. Attendance and hours by subject are kept for your records.

Chores & dinner

A chore chart for each child with points and streaks, and a parent view to see who is behind. A dinner plan shows who cooks and who does the dishes, with reminders.

Today

A quick look at the day for whoever is looking: what is happening now, what needs them, and dinner. Kids see their school and chores first; parents see who needs a look.

And more

Recipes

A family recipe box with photos and search. Open a recipe and scale it up or down for the number of people at the table.

Calendar & events

Family events with Going, Maybe and Can't replies, repeating events and birthdays, with feast days alongside.

Feed & photos

A private family feed with photos, comments and likes, and photo albums. Private groups let grandparents or your parish see only what you choose.

Wall dashboard

On a shared screen, such as an old touch-screen Chromebook on the wall, kids tap to log in quickly, and when nobody is using it a dashboard shows chores and school progress. More on screens and shared devices.

Emergency page

Phone numbers, allergies, doctor and a meeting spot, ready for a sitter or grandparent.

Chat (optional)

Family and group chat is available, but it starts switched off. Why we think most families can leave it off.
