What is inside

Everything your household needs, nothing it doesn't

Every feature has its own On/Off switch, so your family sees only what you choose. Screens show sample family data.

Prayer

A prayer book in English and Latin with search and favorites, a guided Rosary and other guided devotions, and a family prayer list. The liturgical season and feast days appear through the app, with a button to the day's Mass readings.

School

Courses for each child spread over your school days, with assignments, due dates and a week view. Each child sees only their own work, and parents see who is on pace or behind. Attendance fills itself in as lessons are checked off, and you can print a year report for each child.

Chores & dinner

Daily chore lists and printable charts for each child, and a parent view to see who is behind. If you like, add deadlines, extra jobs for points and a family store (how rewards work). A dinner plan shows who cooks and who does the dishes, with reminders.

Today

A quick look at the day for whoever is looking: what is happening now, what needs them, and dinner, under a family photo of your choosing. Kids see their school and chores first; parents see who needs a look.

And more

House rules

A short list of how your family lives together at home, kept where everyone can see it.

Calendar & events

Family events with Going, Maybe and Can't replies, repeating events and birthdays, with feast days alongside.

Feed & photos

A private family feed with up to four photos in a post, comments and likes, and photo albums. Only your household sees it.

Wall dashboard

On a shared screen, such as an old touch-screen Chromebook on the wall, kids tap to log in quickly, and when nobody is using it a dashboard shows chores and school progress. More on screens and shared devices.

Emergency page

Phone numbers, allergies, doctor and a meeting spot, ready for a sitter or grandparent.

Chat (optional)

Family chat is available, but it starts switched off. Why we think most families can leave it off.
