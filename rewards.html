Chores, rewards & the store

Teaching diligence, one chore at a time

Chores are part of family life, and they are also a small school of diligence and honesty. This is how the rewards side of the app works. Every part is optional. Each family switches on only what suits its home, and sets its own times, points and prices.

Everyday chores

The regular jobs, like making the bed or clearing the table, earn no points. They are simply what each of us does for the household. Do them on time and everything carries on as normal.

Deadlines

Each block of chores can have a time it should be done by: morning, midday and evening. Parents choose the times, and some chores, like bedtime, can have none. The switch starts off, so nothing changes until you turn it on.

If one is missed

A late or missed chore is not a disaster. A parent gives a make-up chore, which earns no points, and extras and the store wait until a parent clears it. A child can tap "Done" on the make-up, but only a parent can clear it.

Extra chores

Parents can post extra jobs and set the points for each. A child claims one at a time, does it, and a parent approves it before the points are given.

Points

Points add up to a running balance that carries over from week to week. It never goes below zero. A parent can also mark a child as caught up with school, which lets extras open up.

The daily award

If you like, a child can earn a flat number of points once a day for a full, faithful day. The award is given when all the everyday chores are done on time, there is no make-up waiting, and the child is caught up with school. It is off until you turn it on, and the amount is yours to set.

Double points today

For a special day, a parent can turn on a boost that doubles all points earned that day. It applies to that one day only, then switches itself off.

The family store

Parents set fixed prices for what the family chooses to offer. The store looks like a little shop front, with a product card for each item. Children see what they can afford, and parents see the same cards when they set prices. Ideas to start with:

  • Ice cream
  • Candy
  • Pick the dessert
  • Date night with a parent
  • Extra device time
  • Cash

The items and prices are yours to decide, or you can leave the store off entirely.

Cashing in points

If you offer cash, a child chooses how many points to cash in, and the app shows what that comes to. A parent sets how many points make $1. The starting rate is 4 points per dollar, and you can change it any time.

A note on honesty

The app trusts a child's word when they check off a chore. A short reminder near the check-off asks them to be truthful, because the point is character, not just a tidy house.

Your family decides

These are one family's habits, not rules. You choose which pieces to use, change them any time, or skip them completely.
