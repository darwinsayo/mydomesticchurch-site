Privacy

Your family's information stays your family's

Plain words, no legal fog. Draft: this page will be checked against the final privacy statement before the first families sign up.

What we keep

  • Your sign-up details: family name, your name, email and chosen web address.
  • What your family puts in its site: schedule, prayers, calendar, photos, school records and so on.
  • Your answers to the setup form, which I delete within 30 days after your site is built.
  • If you join the waitlist: your name, email, your children's ages and anything you write in the form. I use it only to invite you, and to send occasional updates if you ticked that box. I delete it if you ask.

Each family is separate

Every family has its own site and its own database. Only the people you give a sign-in to can see your information. Nothing is shared between families unless you choose a feature that does.

What we never do

  • Sell or share your information.
  • Show ads or add tracking to your family's site.
  • Read your family's content, except when you ask me for help.

This website

This public website uses Google Analytics to count visits, so I can see which pages are helpful. It does not reach into your family's site, and nothing your family puts there is ever sent to Google.

Payments

Cards are handled by Stripe. I never see or store your card number.

Leaving

You can leave at any time. Tell me and I will give you a copy of your family's information, then remove your site and data.

Questions

Write to me and I will answer personally.
