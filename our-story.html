Our story

Why I built this

I'll be honest: I'm not nearly as organized as I like to think I am. I'm a stay-at-home dad, and my wife is the academic one. She finished college when some kids were still touring campuses, and I went into the military straight out of high school.

In the early years it was beautiful. We had a few little ones and a curriculum so easy to follow that I could hardly go wrong. Then our family kept growing, and some days the schedule felt like it would snap. We moved, and moved again. Then we came to Kentucky, and not long after, our house burned down.

I never really felt like I caught up after that. Some of our kids fell behind. Our oldest had to fight his way through college, and I carry the weight of knowing my lack of consistency was part of why. That still hurts.

But the kids deserve better than my excuses. So I sat down and built the first version of this app: everything I needed in one place, plus a nudge to get me moving. Within a week we were catching up, and it's been leaps and bounds ever since. I still tear up a little saying that.

Shaping this together

This is a new app, and it's still growing. If you're one of the first families to use it, you're not just a customer. You get a say in how it develops. If something's missing, or something drives you crazy, tell me. Bugs and pain points are what I most want to hear about, and I'll do my best to fix them as they come. I can't promise every feature request will make it in, but every one will be read, and the good ideas get added to the list. That's the nice thing about starting early: we get to build it together.

And if you're behind and feeling it, I hope this helps you find your footing. If you already have a lovely system, maybe just a printer and a good binder, God bless you. I know some of you put this app to shame. This is for the rest of us who are still trying, and still praying our way through it.
