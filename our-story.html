Our story

Why I built this

I'm a stay-at-home dad, and I wasn't nearly as organized as I thought. A growing family, several moves and a house fire broke our routine, and our kids fell behind.

So I built this app for us: everything in one place, with gentle reminders. Within a week we were catching up.

Shaping this together

This is a new app, and it's still growing. If you're one of the first families to use it, you're not just a customer. You get a say in how it develops. If something's missing, or something drives you crazy, tell me. Bugs and pain points are what I most want to hear about, and I'll do my best to fix them as they come. I can't promise every feature request will make it in, but every one will be read, and the good ideas get added to the list. That's the nice thing about starting early: we get to build it together.

And if you're behind and feeling it, I hope this helps you find your footing. If you already have a lovely system, maybe just a printer and a good binder, God bless you. I know some of you put this app to shame. This is for the rest of us who are still trying, and still praying our way through it.

That's also why this app doesn't try to replace paper. Some things just work better on the fridge or in a binder, so whatever can be printed, prints. Keep the old ways that work, and let the new help with the rest. Analog and digital, together.
