Questions

Good questions, plain answers.

Is it only for Catholic families?

It's built with a Catholic family in mind, with the prayer book, readings and wording to match, but any family that likes a private home site is welcome.

Who can see our information?

Only the people you give a sign-in to. Nothing is public, and nothing is shared between families unless you choose a feature that does.

Will this pull my kids toward screens?

I hope not. It is meant to be quick to check and easy to put down, and optional features such as Chat start switched off. Read more.

Do we have to install anything?

No. It opens in your phone's browser, and you can add it to your Home Screen so it looks like an app.

Can we leave at any time?

Yes. Just let me know and I'll give you a copy of your family's information.
