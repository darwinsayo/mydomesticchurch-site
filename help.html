Getting started

What is the My Domestic Church app?

A private home on the web for one Catholic family: the daily schedule, chores, school, prayers, the family calendar, dinners and photos in one place. Each family gets its own address and its own sign-ins. Only the people you add can see anything, and there are no ads.

It works alongside paper, not instead of it. Many families print the schedule and chore cards and use the app to keep track. See everything inside.

How does setup work?

We set up your family's site for you. You pick your family's name and address (such as yourname.mydomesticchurch.org), tell us who is in the family, and we add everyone's sign-in. Then you get a welcome email with your address and a short guide.

A short phone call to walk through it is always on offer, never required.

Where do I start once we're set up?

The first time a parent signs in, a Getting started checklist opens. It walks you through the few things that matter most, one at a time:

  1. Add the app to your Home Screen.
  2. Turn on alerts.
  3. Check everyone can sign in.
  4. Fill in the emergency page.
  5. Set up chores and your daily schedule.

Every step can be skipped and finished later. To open the checklist again, tap the gear, then Getting started, or tap Help at the bottom of any page.

Do we need to download an app?

No. The app opens in your phone's web browser, so there is nothing to download from an app store. Add it to your Home Screen once and it opens like any other app, with its own icon.

On an iPhone or iPad (use Safari):

  1. Open your family's address in Safari and sign in.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. From now on, open it from the new icon.

On an Android phone (use Chrome):

  1. Open your family's address in Chrome and sign in.
  2. Tap the menu (three dots, top right).
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.

It also works on a computer, a tablet or a shared Chromebook. Just open your family's address in the browser.

Signing in

Do my children need an email address?

No. Everyone, children included, gets their own username and password from Mom or Dad. No email, phone number or Apple account is needed.

I forgot my password
  1. On the sign-in page, tap Forgot password?
  2. Type your username and tap Ask for a new password.
  3. Your family's admin (usually Mom or Dad) is told right away. Ask them for your new password, then sign in.

Once you are in, you can change it to one you will remember: tap your picture at the top right.

For parents: how do I give someone a new password?
  1. Tap the gear at the top of the page.
  2. Tap People.
  3. Tap the person, then Reset password.
  4. Tell them the new password. They can change it later from their own picture.

Only the family admin can reset passwords.

For parents: how do I add someone?
  1. Tap the gear, then People.
  2. Tap Add a family member.
  3. Give them a name, a username and a starting password.
  4. Tell them their username and password. They can change the password in their profile.

There are three roles. Admin can do everything, including adding people and resetting passwords. Parent can manage chores, school and the schedule. Member is for children: they see their own day and tick off their own work.

Phone alerts

How do I turn on alerts?

Alerts are the gentle reminders on our phones: whose turn it is for dinner or dishes, new family events, posts and messages. Each person turns them on for their own phone.

  1. On an iPhone, first add the app to your Home Screen (how) and open it from that icon. iPhones only allow alerts from apps on the Home Screen.
  2. Tap your picture at the top right.
  3. Tap Turn on alerts, then Allow when your phone asks.
  4. Below that, choose which kinds of alerts you want, and set quiet hours if you like, so nothing comes at night.

Each phone or tablet is turned on separately. If you use two devices, do it on both.

I'm not getting alerts

Check these in order. One of them is almost always the answer.

  1. On an iPhone, are you opening the app from the Home Screen icon, not from Safari?
  2. Tap your picture. Does it say alerts are on for this phone? If not, tap Turn on alerts.
  3. Are quiet hours covering the time you expected an alert?
  4. In your phone's own Settings, under Notifications, are alerts allowed for the app?

Still nothing? Use Something wrong? or write to us.

Today and the day

What does the Today screen show?

Today is a quick look at the day for whoever is looking. Children see their own day first: school, chores and anything waiting for them. Parents see who needs a look, what is coming up and what is for dinner.

At the top is the day, the liturgical season, and a button to the day's Mass readings.

How do I change the photo at the top of Today?
  1. Tap the gear, then Today screen.
  2. Tap Choose a photo and pick one from your phone or computer.
  3. Tap the small pieces under the preview to show or hide the greeting, the date, the feast day and the readings button.

Without a photo of your own, a built-in picture is used.

How do I change our daily schedule?
  1. Tap the gear, then Daily schedule.
  2. Change, add or remove times: meals, school, prayers, bedtime.

The schedule shows on Today and on the Daily tab. To print it for the fridge, see Printing.

Chores

How do children tick off their chores?
  1. Tap the Daily tab at the bottom.
  2. Tap the circle next to a chore once it is really done.

Children cannot untick a chore. If one was ticked by mistake, a parent can untick it. A new day starts with a clean list on its own.

Younger children who use paper chore cards can hand them to Mom or Dad, who ticks them off in the app.

For parents: how do I set up chores?
  1. Tap the gear, then Chores.
  2. Tap Each person's chores.
  3. Pick a child and add their chores, the time of day and which days.

Start each child with a short list. You can add more any time.

Deadlines, extra chores for points and a family store are there too. They are optional and stay off until you turn them on. How rewards work.

School

For parents: how do I set up school?
  1. Tap the gear, then School.
  2. Add each child's subjects and the books or courses you use. You can spread a course over your school days.
  3. Add your holidays and breaks, so the plan skips them.

You choose the curriculum. The app only keeps track of it.

How do I see who is on pace and who is behind?

Open the School tab. Each child shows how much of today is done and whether they are on pace, ahead or behind, with anything overdue. Tap a child to see the details.

Each child sees only their own work. Attendance fills itself in as lessons are ticked off.

How do I print a year report or records?
  1. Open the School tab.
  2. Tap Records.
  3. Tap Year report next to a child, or print every child's at once.

Records also keeps attendance, hours and grades for the year. In the print screen, choose "Save as PDF" to keep a copy.

Prayer

Where are the prayers and the Rosary?
  1. Tap the Family tab.
  2. Tap Prayers we say.
  3. Tap Start on the Rosary or another devotion to pray it one step at a time.

Tap the star next to a prayer to keep it in your favorites and on Today. The prayer book has the common prayers in English and Latin.

Can we keep a list of who we are praying for?

Yes. Our prayers under the Family tab is your household's own prayer list. Only your family sees it. A parent can turn it on or off from the gear, under Our prayers.

Where do feast days and the readings show?

The liturgical season and today's feast show at the top of Today, with a button to the day's Mass readings. Feast days also appear on the family calendar.

Calendar

How do I add an event?
  1. Tap the Family tab, then Calendar of events.
  2. Tap the round + button.
  3. Add the name, the day and time, and where. You can make it repeat each week, or each year for a birthday.

Everyone can answer Going, Maybe or Can't. Add to my calendar copies an event to your phone's own calendar.

Printing

How do I print chore cards or the schedule?
  1. Tap the Family tab, then Printables.
  2. Pick which children to include.
  3. Tap Print chore cards, Print the chore board or Print the schedule.

Your phone or computer's print screen opens. Choose "Save as PDF" there to keep a copy instead. Printing from a computer is usually easiest.

Settings and features

Where are the settings?

Parents tap the gear at the top of any page. Settings is grouped into your household (people, schedule, school, chores), look and faith, screens and sign-in, and a few more.

Children do not see the gear. Your own name, picture, password and alerts are under your picture at the top right.

Can we turn features off?

Yes. Every optional feature has its own On/Off switch in Settings, and most start off. Your family sees only what you choose. Turning a feature off hides it; nothing is deleted.

Why is Chat off at the start?

We take screen time seriously. Chat starts off for every family, and we suggest leaving it off unless your family truly needs it. The family feed already covers news and photos. If you want it, a parent can turn it on in Settings, under Chat.

Can we use a shared computer or a screen on the wall?

Yes. A shared Chromebook or tablet works well for children to tick off chores and school. Each child signs in with their own name. A parent can also set up a wall or kitchen screen that shows the chore board; the gear has the details under Screens and sign-in. More on screens.

Privacy and billing

Who can see our family's things?

Only the people your family adds. Each family has its own site and its own data, kept apart from every other family. Nothing is sold, shared or used for ads.

We don't look at your family's things unless you ask us for help. Read the privacy page.

Is our data backed up?

Yes. A copy of everything is made every week. The family admin can also make one any time: tap the gear, then Backups.

How much does it cost?

$10 a month for the whole family, however many children you have. The first 30 days are free. There is no setup fee.

Billing, cancelling or a copy of our data

Write to Darwin and he will take care of it himself. A family that leaves can ask for a copy of everything it has stored.

Something not working?

First, a few quick fixes
  1. Close the app completely and open it again. This also picks up the newest version.
  2. Check you have a signal or Wi-Fi.
  3. Sign out and back in (tap your picture).
How do I report a problem?
  1. Tap Something wrong? at the bottom of any page in the app.
  2. Say what happened and where you were. Add a screenshot if you like.
  3. Tap Send to the parents.

Children can use it too. It goes to Mom and Dad first, who see it in Settings under Problem reports. If they can't sort it out, they write to us.

Still stuck? Write to us

Write to Darwin. He reads every message and answers himself. It helps to say which phone you use and what you were trying to do.
