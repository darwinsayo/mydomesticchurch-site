You asked for it

Okay, here is everything

Grab a coffee. Every feature has its own On/Off switch, so you only get what you turn on. And we keep adding more.

Today

One screen, shaped by who is holding the phone.

  • A Today screen that changes for whoever is looking: kids see their day, parents see what needs them
  • A “Next up” timeline, so nobody asks “what’s next?” for the ninth time
  • Tonight’s dinner, with arrows to peek at other days
  • The feast day and liturgical season, front and center
  • Each person’s starred favorite prayers
  • A one-page fridge sheet for the analog among us

Daily

Chores, schedules and dinner duty, minus the nagging.

  • A family schedule in a small window that opens up when you tap the arrow
  • A daily routine for each child
  • Chores with points and streaks
  • A parent view that shows who is behind
  • A dinner plan: who cooks, who does the dishes, and reminders

School

Because the paperwork should not eat the school year.

  • Courses for each child, spread over your school days
  • “On pace” or “behind” at a glance
  • Assignments with due dates and repeats
  • A week view
  • Holidays and breaks
  • Attendance and hours by subject, kept for your records
  • Each child sees only their own work
  • Your own school name, saint, motto and crest, Homeschool or Academy

Feed

Grandma gets the photos. Strangers get nothing.

  • A private family feed with photos, comments and likes
  • Photo albums
  • Private groups, so grandparents, cousins or your parish see only what you choose

Prayer

The part we are most fond of.

  • A prayer book in English and Latin, with search and favorites
  • A starter prayer book of about 20 standard Catholic prayers, including the Chaplet of Divine Mercy and St. Michael, that a parent can add to, edit and reorder
  • A guided Rosary: the app picks the day’s mysteries (Joyful, Luminous, Sorrowful or Glorious), and you can switch sets
  • The Rosary in English or Latin, one prayer at a time, tapping Next after each Hail Mary like moving to the next bead
  • A family prayer list where each request stays seven nights unless you renew it
  • Nightly prayers for the whole family
  • Prayers picked for each person on their Today screen
  • The liturgical season shown in color across the app
  • A button to the day’s Mass readings

Together

The everyday stuff of a busy house.

  • A recipe box with photos and search, and recipes that scale to the number at your table
  • Family events with Going, Maybe and Can’t replies
  • Repeating events, birthdays and feast days on the calendar
  • An emergency page for the sitter or grandparent: numbers, allergies, doctor and meeting spot

Settings and safety

You are in charge. Always.

  • An On/Off switch for every feature, so your family sees only what you choose
  • Personal logins with no email address needed
  • Roles: Admin, Parent and Member
  • A “Something wrong?” button for kids (and testers) to speak up
  • Private by design: your own family site, no ads, no tracking
